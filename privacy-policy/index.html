Leaf & Ledger Limited Privacy Statement

Leaf & Ledger Limited (“Leaf & Ledger Limited”, the “Firm”, “we” or “us”) is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This statement explains what personal information we process in connection with this website and when you contact us, and the rights you have.

Who we are

Leaf & Ledger Limited, a private company limited by shares. Registered in Ireland, company number 766181. Registered office: 20 Harcourt Street, Dublin 2, Ireland. Leaf & Ledger Limited is the data controller for the personal information described in this statement. You can contact us at evan@leafandledger.eu.

This website

This website is a static site. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, advertising tools, contact forms or embedded third-party content, and we do not collect personal information through it.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not have access to these logs. GitHub may process this information outside the European Economic Area; please see GitHub’s Privacy Statement for the safeguards it applies.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your name, email address, organisation and the content of your message to respond to you. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about the Firm and its published research. We keep this correspondence only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law. Our email is provided by Google Workspace, which processes messages on our behalf.

We do not sell, rent or otherwise make personal information commercially available to any third party.

Links to other sites

This website contains links to other websites, such as LinkedIn. Those websites have their own privacy policies and we do not accept any responsibility or liability for them. Please check those policies before you submit any personal information to those websites.

Your rights regarding your personal information

The European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation and other applicable data protection laws provide certain rights for data subjects. You are entitled to request details of the information we hold about you and how we process it. You may also have a right to have it rectified or deleted, to restrict or object to our processing of it, and in some circumstances to have it transferred to another organisation. To make a request, email evan@leafandledger.eu. We will respond as soon as practicable and in any event within one month of receipt. We may request proof of identification to verify your request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, which in Ireland is the Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this Privacy Statement

We may change this Privacy Statement from time to time. If we do, we will post the changes here and update the date below.
