ABOUT US

Leaf & Ledger is a European research firm serving institutional investors and managers in real assets and private markets.

Founded in 2024, the firm’s work has focused on valuation signals, market structure, and execution topics that translate technical assessment into underwriting and strategy.

The firm’s research archive, covering capital markets, private credit, energy transition, and regulatory developments, remains publicly available.
