Evan G. Campbell, CFA

Founder

  • 20+ years of buy-side institutional investment experience in real assets and private markets
  • Expertise in Portfolio & Risk Management, Acquisitions, Structured Transactions, Special Situations, and Capital Raising
  • Former head of Portfolio Management for Kennedy Wilson in Ireland (>€3bn AUM)
  • Experience at Ontario Teachers’ Pension Plan, Avestus Capital Partners, and Granite REIT
  • Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)
  • CFA Institute Climate Risk, Valuation & Investing Certification
  • Education and coursework at:
    • University of British Columbia
    • Harvard University
    • CFA Institute

Evan brings a practitioner lens from front-office roles to independent research built for investment committees and portfolio leads. His approach is numbers-first and decision-oriented, with a focus on areas where transition and climate risk are financially material to underwriting, portfolio planning, and execution.

Evan’s career spans Europe and the Americas, initially trained at one of Canada’s leading institutional investors. His experience covers technical modelling, portfolio management, structured transactions, capital raising, and risk management across real assets and related capital structures.

Prior to founding Leaf & Ledger, Evan was Head of Portfolio Management for Kennedy Wilson’s portfolio of more than €3 billion in Ireland. He also held roles at Ontario Teachers’ Pension Plan, Avestus Capital Partners, and Granite REIT.

His academic credentials include a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of British Columbia, the CFA designation, and the CFA Institute Climate Risk, Valuation & Investing Certificate, along with strategic management coursework from Harvard Extension School and specialist training from Moody’s UK Investor Training Service and The Marquee Group in LBO modelling.
